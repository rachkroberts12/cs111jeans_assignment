Welcome

My name is Rachel-Kate, and I have tons of creative hobbies including photography, drawing, theater, wood-working, architecture, and interior design. I also enjoy hiking and exploring nature since I am a wildlife photographer in my free time.
I love exploring new places and hiking off the beaten trail to get good photos, even if that means waiting around. In fact, as I write this, I am actively in the Rocky Mountains uploading my newest batch of wildlife photos to my computer.
Something I also love doing is being a photographer for churches and missions. Being a photographer for Christian camps and missions trips is something I've gotten the opportunity to do a couple of times but would love to do more of. In fact, you may recognize me as the chapel photographer for Huntington University, a position I've been doing for 2 years.

My career is an art teacher, but I still love to do nature photography in my free time. My degree is K-12, but I prefer to teach highschool due to the level of projects I can work with. In fact, I am already an art teacher in my hometown of Warsaw, IN. I teach K-8th every day, all at once, in a non-profit kids center.
